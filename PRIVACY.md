# Lumen Reader – Privacy Policy

Lumen Reader is an offline document reader.

- It does **not** collect, store or transmit any personal data.
- Documents are opened locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.
- The only data the app saves are your preferences (theme, language, zoom, window size)
  in your local application data folder. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- Links inside documents that point to websites are opened in your default browser
  only when you click them.

Contact: please open an issue in this repository.
